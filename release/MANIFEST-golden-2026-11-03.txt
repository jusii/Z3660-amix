  ethernet   eth_adv1000 defaults to NO: the PHY advertises 10/100 only (07c4602). With a real 060 at
             1000 Mbit the GEM's RX FIFO overruns on 4+ back-to-back full-size frames (A4000D: overruns =
             lost frames, 67/80 echoes); at 100 Mbit 80/80 and no overrun. eth_adv1000 YES keeps gigabit.
  The template and the SD-content z3660cfg.txt say eth_adv1000 NO.
